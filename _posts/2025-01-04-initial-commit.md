# Initial commit

Identifies īśvara as both the material and intelligent causes of the universe (BrSūBh 1.1.2). Īśvara emanates... auto-cosmogony, making īśvara the material of the universe.

<https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/shankara/>{:target='_blank'}

- Consciousness cannot become an objective content of consciousness
- Consciousness is self-evident
- One cannot deny their own consciousness
- Consciousness thus falls outside the scope of negation
- It does not require a second awareness for it to be known
- It is self-established
- It does not require a means of knowledge to be known, nor any proof or justification for its existence
- It is self-disclosing. No second thing mediates its access to itself
- The self-illumination of witnessing consciousness accounts for the immediacy of cognition without falling into an infinite regress of mental modes
- It resists qualification, eliminative reduction, or dependence on a second thing
- We mutually superimpose consciousness on to our mind-body, and the mind-body on to consciousness
